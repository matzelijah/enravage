# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026. Enravage (the "App") is provided by Elijah J. Matz ("we", "us").

## The short version
Enravage analyses your photos **entirely on your iPhone**. Your photos, face data, the text it reads in photos, your ratings and everything else it learns **are never sent to us or to any third party**. We have no servers that receive your data, no accounts, and no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and the App sends nothing to any artificial-intelligence service: every model runs on your device. **We do not collect, sell or share personal data.**

## What the App accesses, with your permission
- **Your photo library.** You choose Full Access or Limited Access (selected photos only), and can change this at any time in iOS Settings. The App reads photos to analyse them. It never edits, moves or deletes them.
- **Adding to Photos.** Only when you save a post, to add those photos to an album named "Enravage" in your Photos library.

## What happens on your device
All of the following runs on your iPhone, in the App, and nothing from it is uploaded:
- **Photo analysis:** quality, sharpness, light, colour, look-alike groups, categories and themes, using Apple's frameworks and models built into the App.
- **Your eye and your angles:** what you Keep and Pass in Rate trains a small model, on your device, that predicts which photos you'll like. If you have marked yourself in People, it also learns from the head angle, size and position of your face in photos of you.
- **Private-info check:** the App reads text in some photos, using Apple's on-device text recognition, to spot things like cards, IDs, email addresses, phone numbers, licence plates and readable screens. The text is processed in memory only and **never stored**: only a yes/no result and a general reason (for example "email or phone number") are kept.
- **Nudity blurring:** if Blur nudity is on and you have turned on Sensitive Content Warning in iOS Settings, the App asks Apple's on-device nudity detection about some photos. Only a yes/no result is kept. Nothing is sent to Apple or to us.
- **Set aside and bring back:** the App leaves some photos out of its suggestions (documents, very blurry or very dark shots, look-alikes). If you bring photos back, it compares them, on your device, with the photos still left out to suggest others like them.
- **People (face data), only if you turn it on:** see "Face data" below.

## What the App stores, and where
Only inside the App on your device. The App's database (everything below) is excluded from iPhone and iCloud backups; your on/off settings may be included in your own device backup.
- identifiers of photos in your library and the scores, tags, groups and results the App calculates for them;
- your ratings, picks, saved posts, settings, the filters you choose, and the photos you bring back from Set aside;
- if People is on: face data (below) and the names and relationships you give people.

## Face data (People)
People is **off until you turn it on**, and turning it on asks for your consent first.
- **What:** the position, head angle and a numeric description of each face found in your photos (a list of numbers that cannot be turned back into a picture), and the names you give.
- **Why:** only to group faces, recognise the people you name, and filter and pick your photos by person, for you, on your device.
- **Where:** only in the App on your device. Never uploaded, shared, sold, or used to train any model outside your device.
- **How long, and how it is destroyed:** face data is kept only while People is on. It is deleted immediately and permanently from the device when you turn People off, when you use Settings → Delete all app data, or when you delete the App, whichever comes first. We never hold a copy.

## Security
The App is built so that your data never leaves your device, which is the strongest protection there is: there is nothing on our side to breach.
- **Isolated:** iOS keeps the App's data in its own sandbox; other apps cannot read or change it.
- **Encrypted:** the App's data is stored with iOS Data Protection, encrypted with a key tied to your device passcode. After the iPhone restarts it cannot be read until you unlock the device. Shared copies of photos use the strictest protection.
- **Not backed up or transmitted:** the App's database, including any face data, is excluded from iPhone and iCloud backups and never sent anywhere.
- **Minimal:** text read in photos is never stored, and face data exists only while People is on.
- **You stay in control:** turning People off deletes all face data; Settings → Delete all app data or deleting the App deletes everything.

No system is perfectly secure. Protecting the device itself (a passcode, current iOS updates, not jailbreaking it, not installing untrusted profiles) is outside the App's control; on a jailbroken or otherwise compromised device, other software may be able to read any app's data.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** We cannot see your photos, scores, faces, names, ratings or anything else in the App.

**Crash reports.** If you have chosen in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics (for example, which part of the App crashed and the iOS version). These come from Apple, under Apple's privacy policy, and contain no photos, face data, text from photos or names. You can turn this off in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

## Sharing
The App shares nothing by itself. When **you** choose Post or Share, the App prepares copies of the photos you picked, with location and camera details removed, and hands them to the iOS share sheet; what happens next is up to you and the app you share to, under that app's own privacy policy.

## iCloud
- If your photos are stored in iCloud, iOS may download previews or full photos so the App can show and analyse them. This happens between your device and Apple, under Apple's privacy terms; we never receive the photos.
- The "Enravage" album you save to is part of your Photos library, so if you use iCloud Photos it syncs to your other devices like any other album.

## Children and age information
The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children, because we collect none. The App does not ask for your age and does not receive your age range or a parent's consent status from the App Store.

## Your rights
Because we do not collect or hold any of your personal data, we have nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side: everything is on your device and under your control. You can delete it at any time with **Settings → Delete all app data** or by deleting the App, and turn People off to delete all face data. If you live in a place with privacy laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA, you keep all your rights under them; contact us with any question.

## Changes
If this policy changes in a material way, the App will ask you to review it before you continue.
