# Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[support@enravage.com](mailto:support@enravage.com?subject=Enravage%20support)**. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**Does Enravage upload my photos?**
No. Every photo is analysed on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account or server.

**Will it delete or change my photos?**
Never. Enravage doesn't edit, move or delete anything. Photos you save are added to an album called "Enravage" in Photos.

**Why are Picks not great yet?**
Rate about 20 photos in the Rate tab (Keep or Pass). The more you rate, the better Picks matches your eye.

**Some real photos were set aside as junk, blurry or dark.**
Open the photo and tap **Show anyway**, or in Settings → What Enravage set aside, turn a whole category back on.

**The first analysis is slow.**
Big libraries take a while the first time. Keep Enravage open, or leave your iPhone charging: it finishes in the background.

**How do I delete my data?**
Settings → Delete all app data, or delete the app. Your photos stay untouched.

**Enravage only sees some of my photos.**
iPhone Settings → Apps → Enravage → Photos → **Full Access**.
